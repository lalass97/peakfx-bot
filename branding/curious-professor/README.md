# The Curious Professor — YouTube Banner

Precise banner template for YouTube.

- Canvas: **2560 × 1440**
- Centered mobile/tablet safe area: **1546 × 423**
- All critical text, icons, and the professor placeholder are kept inside the safe area.

Open `banner-template.svg` in a browser, Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape, or Canva and export to PNG at 2560 × 1440.

Replace the professor placeholder with the approved professor artwork without moving it outside the safe zone.
