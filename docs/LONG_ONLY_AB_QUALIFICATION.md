# Long-only A/B qualification policy

The long-only candidate is an isolated repair experiment, not an assumed fix.

## Required evidence

The unchanged baseline and long-only candidate must use the same:

- EURUSD H1 history and date range;
- broker/tester model and starting deposit;
- spread, commission, and slippage assumptions;
- stop, target, risk, session, cooldown, and safety settings;
- reporting and qualification pipeline.

Only direction eligibility may differ.

## Promotion rule

The candidate may be promoted for further demo research only when all gates pass:

1. At least 300 completed candidate trades.
2. At least 35% of the baseline trade count retained.
3. Positive cost-inclusive net profit.
4. Net profit better than the unchanged baseline.
5. Profit factor at least 1.20.
6. At least 60% of tested calendar years profitable.
7. Maximum drawdown no worse than the baseline.
8. Positive net profit under doubled trading costs.
9. Green sequence-risk qualification.

A failure is a rejection. Missing sample or sequence evidence is inconclusive, never green.

## Restrictions

This gate does not authorize live trading. It does not permit changing hours, stops, targets, risk, or other filters during the same comparison. A candidate that passes remains subject to untouched out-of-sample and demo-forward validation.
